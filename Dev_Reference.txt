If you want complete feature lists, go to manuscript in scope section.

Features:
- Fish Species Identification via Computer-Vision
- Offline Species Library
- Map w/ Restricted Areas from government data source
- User Activity Log
- Notifications regarding violations, distance, and new zone restrictions.

Scope:
AI-based identification of selected fish species: The system can identify a species of a fish if it exists inside its dataset.
Offline fish species library.
GPS-based location monitoring.
Map of selected government-provided restricted areas.
Geofencing and location-based warnings.
Notifications about selected fishing regulations and closed seasons.
User-friendly interface for municipal fishers.
